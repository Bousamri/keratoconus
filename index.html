Keratoconus in the Middle East and North Africa

Projected number of people affected in 21 countries, 2025 to 2050

An estimated 13.1 million people aged 10 and over had keratoconus across the region in 2025. By 2050 the number is projected to reach 18.5 million, an increase of 41% that comes from population growth and age structure alone. Thirteen of the 21 countries have no population-based prevalence study of their own, and five of them carry nearly half of the projected 2050 burden.

How these numbers were made

A single pooled prevalence of 2.34% (95% CI 1.61 to 3.39), from 13 population-based studies of 19,746 people, was applied to each country's United Nations projected population aged 10 and over. The figures therefore do not reflect country-specific prevalence, which could not be estimated for most countries. Differences between countries come from population size and age structure, not from any difference in disease frequency.

The protocol was registered on PROSPERO. Full methods, sensitivity analyses and the age-structured projections are in the full paper, which is under peer review at Cornea.
